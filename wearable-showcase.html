01 · Development timeline

From first idea to where it stands today

  1. Phase 01[MONTH YEAR]
    Concept

    The sleep-aware remote

    Problem identified: falling asleep with the TV, lights or AC still running. The idea: a wrist device that knows when you've dozed off and turns things off for you over infrared.

  2. Phase 02[MONTH YEAR]
    Hardware prototype

    First working prototype

    Motion and heart-rate sensing on a [MICROCONTROLLER] board, with rule-based sleep-onset detection driving an IR LED transmitter. [ADD PROTOTYPE DETAILS]

  3. Phase 03[MONTH YEAR]
    IR integration

    Talking to the room

    Storing IR codes for surrounding devices and testing range and reliability in a real bedroom. [ADD TEST RESULTS]

  4. Phase 04[MONTH YEAR]
    Pivot

    Becoming a fitness tracker

    The sensors needed for sleep detection could do far more. Scope widened to activity, steps and continuous heart-rate tracking. [WHY THE PIVOT HAPPENED]

  5. Phase 05[MONTH YEAR]
    AI model

    Training on sleep apnea data

    A model trained on [DATASET NAME] recordings from sleep apnea patients learns to recognise disrupted breathing patterns from wrist-level signals.

  6. Phase 06 · Now[MONTH YEAR]
    Current build

    AI health tracker

    Fitness tracking, sleep monitoring and AI-based apnea screening in one device, with sleep-triggered IR control kept as a core feature.
